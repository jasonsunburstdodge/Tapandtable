Questions

About the Scorecard

What is the Tap and Table Scorecard?

A free, instant, first-look read on how your restaurant or bar's concept and location are likely shaping foot traffic, competitive differentiation, guest appeal, and cost exposure — scored across eleven categories on the same 0–10 scale used in our full engagements.

Is the Scorecard the same as a full audit?

No. The Scorecard is a cursory, surface-level read that shows you where things stand. It intentionally does not explain why, or what to do about it — that's what our Deep-Dive Analysis is built to deliver.

What information do I need to run a Scorecard?

Just the restaurant or bar's name, its concept, and its location. The Scorecard runs instantly from there.

How much does the Scorecard cost?

Nothing. It's free. Our full Deep-Dive Analysis, which explains why each score landed where it did and what to do about it, is a one-time $1,200 engagement that includes a 1-hour findings review with our team.
